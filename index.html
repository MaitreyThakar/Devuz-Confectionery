Common Questions

How many days in advance should I order?
We bake everything fresh to order. For regular cakes or cupcakes, please order at least 2-3 days in advance. For customized 3D theme cakes or multi-tier wedding cakes, we recommend 1-2 weeks notice.
Do you offer 100% eggless options?
Yes, absolutely! We specialize in 100% pure vegetarian and eggless baking. Our eggless cakes are incredibly soft, moist, and delicious—you won't even taste the difference.
Do you deliver outside Junagadh?
For our delicate custom cakes, we currently hand-deliver only within Junagadh city limits to ensure they arrive in perfect condition. However, if you are ordering other treats (like cookies, dry cakes, or chocolates) from anywhere else within Gujarat, we can make it happen! Please contact us 4-5 days in advance so we can arrange secure shipping.
